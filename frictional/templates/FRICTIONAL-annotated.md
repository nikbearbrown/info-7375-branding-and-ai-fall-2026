## Dated entry

**Date** *(mark retrospective entries — e.g. `2026-10-03 (retrospective, written 10-05)`)*:

**What I tried and expected:**
*→ Attempts (2 pts). The attempt AND the prediction. A wrong prediction scores the same as a right one; a missing one caps the row at 1.*

**What happened / what I found difficult or checked:**
*→ Friction and response (2 pts). Name the object — a value, a test, a line, a filename. "It was confusing" names a category, not an experience. A check that found nothing counts.*

**What I did next:**
*→ Friction and response. Include what you abandoned and why. Dead ends are worth more than the fix.*

**What Claude or another person contributed:**
*→ Human and AI contributions (2 pts). Say what you accepted, changed, or rejected. Naming the tool alone is a 1. "No help this session" is a complete answer.*

**What I understand now / still do not understand:**
*→ Learning and uncertainty (2 pts). Name something still open. "Nothing unresolved" is almost never true.*

**Evidence, commit, and next step:**
*→ Traceable process (2 pts). A test name, a commit subject, an output file, a transcript — something a reviewer can open.*
