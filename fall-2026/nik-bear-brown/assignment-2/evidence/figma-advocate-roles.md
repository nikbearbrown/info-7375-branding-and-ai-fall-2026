# Figma — the Advocate roles only

## Executive summary

> Copy for Assignment 2. The maintained version is [`advocate/figma-advocate-roles.md`](../../advocate/figma-advocate-roles.md); this copy is frozen with the Part 1 evidence and will be redone when Part 1 is rewritten on the assignment page.

**What this is.** The narrowed Figma list: of the 152 jobs Figma had open on September 19, 2026, the three that are Advocate roles, plus the five "enablement" roles that are the nearest neighbours. Everything else — 144 engineering, sales, and operations postings — is left out on purpose. This is the page to read if you only want to know *what Figma is hiring an advocate to do*.

**Why read it.** The wider lists exist and are linked below, but they answer a different question ("what mentions my skills?"). This one answers "which Figma jobs are the job I want?" — and the answer fits on one screen. It also shows the split I did not expect: Figma's advocates sit in **Marketing**, and its enablement roles sit in **Sales**. Same verbs (teach, demo, write, present), different departments, different audiences — community versus paying customers.

**What it found.** Three Designer Advocate postings: one in US hubs (my Assignment 2 dream job, $153K–$317K, posted September 1), one in San Francisco focused on partner certification programs (posted July 13), one in London (posted July 23). All three are full-time, hub-based, in Marketing → Advocacy. The five Customer/Field Enablement roles are all outside the US (Berlin, London, Paris, São Paulo, Tokyo ×2) and belong to Sales. **There is no US-remote advocate role at Figma today**, and no part-time or contract one.

**Status.** The US Designer Advocate is kept. Partnerships is under consideration. London and the enablement roles are passes on geography or department. Verdicts on the wider 55-job match list are still pending.
