## Market awareness: what the postings actually ask for

Researched from the 58 kept postings of the 2026-09-26 Lectern run (1,891 postings across six boards). The skills that appear most frequently in education-advocate and developer-relations roles:

1. **Written, visual, and video teaching material about the product** — tutorials, docs, sample apps, workshops.
2. **On-camera presence, your own voice** — Writer's posting explicitly rules out AI-generated voice.
3. **Hands-on tool depth** — Figma design systems, tokens, and Dev Mode; production Webflow sites; sample code.
4. **Community and public speaking** — workshops, office hours, conference talks.
5. **AI-assisted workflows** — MCP servers, evals, shipping with AI tools in the loop.

Both technical requirements (tool depth, sample code) and soft skills (teaching, community) show up in every posting. The emerging trend is AI-assisted everything: the tools are expected, the teaching is the differentiator.

## Three areas to strengthen this semester

**1. Figma design systems with tokens and Dev Mode handoff.** The job search named this as the blocking gap: every Figma-side posting requires shipping a component library with design tokens and working with engineers in Dev Mode, and I have never done it. Assignment 3 closes exactly this gap. Two to three sentences is the requirement; the reason is simple — without it, the applications the whole project is aimed at fail on the stated requirements.

**2. Brand strategy and positioning.** I can build the tools and run the workshops, but I have never written the strategy that turns a tool into a pitch — which audience, which claim, which proof. This course is that work, and the A5 Conductor Brief is its artifact. It matters because the advocate role doesn't get posted; it gets proposed, and a proposal is a branding document.

**3. On-camera teaching samples.** The postings ask for video explicitly and rule out synthetic voice, and my current film work is narrated rather than on-camera. A three-to-five-minute sample teaching one design-system idea would close the loop. It matters because "can teach on camera" is a stated requirement I currently evidence only by assertion.
