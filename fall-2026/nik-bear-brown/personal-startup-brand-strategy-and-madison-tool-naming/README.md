## Part 1A — Brand foundation (15 pts)

Drafted from the teaching philosophy in the facts file (record): *active engagement over passive consumption; AI-enhanced personalized learning; real-world problem solving; collaborative innovation; ethical AI development.* The wording is a judgment.

- **Mission (what).** I build and teach AI tools that people can check, and I show my work while I do it.
- **Purpose (why).** `[TODO: DEFINE]` his reason, in his words. A draft to react to: because a polished answer is not the same as a trustworthy one, and students and professionals both need to practise telling them apart.
- **Vision (where).** AI education that reaches learners beyond one campus, built with the companies whose tools are taught, with every method open for anyone to reproduce.
- **Values (how), draft of four:** *learn by doing*; *show the evidence* (every claim traceable to a saved source); *check the machine*; *credit honestly* (say what the AI did and what the human decided).

**UVP iterations (judgment):**

1. I help education teams build AI courses through experience.
2. I help companies teach their AI and design tools to universities and learners through courses I have already built and tested at scale.
3. I help vendors teach their tools well: I have delivered a 700+ learner course on working with Claude, built 25+ course assistants, and developed a 13-module public course.
4. I help companies whose product needs teaching make the learning materials and workshops, and I show the evidence behind every claim.

**Final UVP (draft):** *I help companies that need their tools taught turn them into courses, workshops and materials that work, with the evidence for every claim.*
