# AI policy for NEU course work

[AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz)

AI assistance is allowed and expected. This course uses Claude Code and Python. Refine, test, and understand the work you submit. Credit starting repositories, source passages, collaborators, and AI assistance; identify what you contributed and what Claude contributed. A generated first draft is a starting point for inspection and improvement.

The instructor or TA may ask you to explain code, methods, results, and decisions. Preserve original predictions and evidence. Distinguish actual executions, simulations, fixtures, and unsupported claims. AI may organize your authentic effort notes; it must not invent struggle, timestamps, understanding, or human approvals.

Each Assignment has 60 implementation points, 10 for an honest Frictional log, 10 for proper GitHub posting, and 20 for relative quality after all submissions are reviewed. Meeting requirements does not guarantee the comparative 20 points. AI use alone is not misconduct; contribution, understanding, and evidence remain your responsibility.

An explainer video is optional. Brutalist is an optional tool. A clearer, more professional explanation may compare better with peers, but tool choice, spending, and the mere existence of a video earn no bonus. A submission without video can earn full credit. No public YouTube upload is required.

This course adaptation carries forward Professor Bear's policy from the linked instructor-supplied video and the prior Prompt Engineering course prerequisites. Canvas provides official institutional policies and section administration.
