# Frictional — 10 points

[AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz)

An honest log of effort and learning, kept in FRICTIONAL.md. It is not a timesheet or a performance of suffering. Short dated entries should connect actual attempts and decisions to evidence. Mark retrospective entries honestly.

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Attempts | 2 | What you actually tried and expected. |
| Friction and response | 2 | Confusion, surprise, a difficult decision, or an assumption you checked, and what you did next. |
| Human and AI contributions | 2 | Your work, Claude's assistance, and what you accepted, changed, or rejected. |
| Learning and uncertainty | 2 | What changed in your understanding, or what remains unresolved. |
| Traceable process | 2 | Dated entries connected to commits, prompts, outputs, drafts, or tests. |
| Total | 10 | |

Per row: 2 specific and sufficient; 1 vague or incomplete; 0 absent. An unsuccessful attempt can earn full credit. If work was straightforward, say so and name your checks. More time, mistakes, or commits do not earn extra points. AI can edit your notes, but you supply the actual experience. See the [template](../templates/FRICTIONAL.md).
