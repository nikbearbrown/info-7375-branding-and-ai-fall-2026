# Relative Quartile — 20 points

[AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz)

Assigned after all submissions for the same Assignment are reviewed. Compare specificity, refinement, demonstrated understanding, inspectable execution evidence, honest limits, and professional communication.

| Quartile | Points | Evidence of relative quality |
|---|---:|---|
| Top 25% | 16–20 | Strongest supported, specific, refined, understandable work. |
| Second 25% | 10–15 | Strong evidence and understanding with less depth or refinement. |
| Third 25% | 5–9 | Limited development, thin evidence, or hard-to-follow communication. |
| Bottom 25% | 0–4 | Largest gaps in support, understanding, completeness, or usability. |

Placement alone does not imply misconduct. Reviewers explain the score within its band. The instructor publishes how ties, uneven group sizes, late work, and resubmissions are handled before grading. Do not rank using commit counts, hours, presumed effort, or tool spending.

Videos and Brutalist are optional. If a peer's explanation is clearer and more professional, that quality can affect comparison; using a named tool or including a video does not automatically change rank. A concise written report with clear evidence can receive full credit. Polished unsupported claims remain unsupported.

Frictional assesses effort; this category compares the resulting work. Implementation and GitHub delivery retain their separate 60 and 10 points. These 20 points are part of the 100-point total, never added on top.
