# Assignment 5 — Campaign and content

**INFO 7375 · Branding and AI · Fall 2026 · 100 points · Due: Canvas**

[AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz)

**Predict → Build It → Use It → Ship It → Verify**

Bundle [Lesson 8](../../lessons/08-campaign-and-content/README.md) into one contribution to your running brand project. The lesson Assessments are ungraded.

## Predict

Which channel adaptation risks changing the meaning of your campaign promise? Save the expectation before the relevant output or feedback.

## Build It

Build a Python campaign-manifest validator for audience, channel, objective, message, claim evidence, owner, and approval status. Produce a small set of draft channel artifacts.

## Use It

Ask Claude to adapt a single campaign brief for three channels. Label paid, earned, shared, and owned placements as plans, not evidence that coverage or purchases occurred.

## Ship It

Submit campaign brief, manifest, three draft messages, and consistency audit. Include the implementation, tests, inputs, actual outputs, source credits, and [submission records](../../templates/README.md). Post the final checked version to the designated GitHub location and upload the matching version to Canvas.

## Verify

Test missing claim proof and contradictory offers across channels. Verify all dates, prices, and promises in the mock campaign. No ad spend, outreach, or publication is required.

Document actual findings and limitations. Fictional cases, simulations, and proposed campaigns must remain labeled. No ad spend, public release, invented testimonial, or legal clearance is required or implied.

## Division of labor

**AI should** adapt messages and inspect consistency.

**Human should** choose the campaign objective, approve actual claims, and authorize any real release.

Explain deductions. The technical handoff row assesses usability and understanding; GitHub points assess delivery. Frictional assesses the actual process, not whether the result succeeded. A finding that survives the audit can earn full credit when checks are discriminating and scope is honest.

An explainer video or Brutalist production is optional and may support communication quality within Relative Quartile. It has no separate points; excellent written evidence can earn full credit. Claude Code is assumed; direct API calls and paid media tools are unnecessary.
