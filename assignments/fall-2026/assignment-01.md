# Assignment 1 — Brand promise and audience

**INFO 7375 · Branding and AI · Fall 2026 · 100 points · Due: Canvas**

[AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz)

**Predict → Build It → Use It → Ship It → Verify**

Bundle [Lesson 1](../../lessons/01-brand-as-evidence/README.md) and [Lesson 2](../../lessons/02-audience-and-research/README.md) into one contribution to your running brand project. The lesson Assessments are ungraded.

## Predict

Which promise will distinguish your running brand, and what evidence would make an audience believe it? Which audience segment has the strongest reason to use the offering? Predict one behavior that would challenge your choice. Save the expectation before the relevant output or feedback.

## Build It

Build a Python brand-brief validator for audience, problem, promise, evidence, alternatives, exclusions, and owner. Choose a personal, fictional, or authorized organizational brand to carry through the course.

Build a Python audience-evidence ledger linking segment, job, observation, source, date, and confidence. Keep observed findings separate from hypotheses.

## Use It

Use Claude Code to draft two competing briefs after your initial prediction. Separate brand assets from claims about audience behavior. Preserve sources and assumptions.

Ask Claude to propose interview questions and challenge your segment assumptions. Use public sources, supplied fixtures, or actual voluntary low-stakes feedback; label synthetic personas and do not invent interviews.

## Ship It

Submit brand-brief.json, validator, two alternatives, and a decision note; audience ledger, question guide, segment comparison, and evidence limits. Include the implementation, tests, inputs, actual outputs, source credits, and [submission records](../../templates/README.md). Post the final checked version to the designated GitHub location and upload the matching version to Canvas.

## Verify

Reject missing audience or evidence. Show a structurally complete but unsupported promise. A polished logo or complete schema does not demonstrate brand equity.

Test missing sources and duplicated observations. Trace one audience claim to its source. Repeated model-generated personas do not establish market demand.

Document actual findings and limitations. Fictional cases, simulations, and proposed campaigns must remain labeled. No ad spend, public release, invented testimonial, or legal clearance is required or implied.

## Division of labor

**AI should** draft alternatives and flag missing evidence; organize sources and propose research questions.

**Human should** choose the running project, justify its promise, and own its claims; choose the segment, conduct any real interaction, and interpret the evidence.
