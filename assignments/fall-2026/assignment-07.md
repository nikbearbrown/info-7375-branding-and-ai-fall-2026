Bundle [Lesson 10](../../lessons/10-governance-and-change/README.md) and [Lesson 11](../../lessons/11-purpose-and-proof/README.md) into one contribution to your running brand project. The lesson Assessments are ungraded.

## Predict

Which small change will create unintended drift across your existing assets? Which values or sustainability claim in your draft is least well supported? Predict the qualification it needs. Save the expectation before the relevant output or feedback.

## Build It

Build a Python asset manifest with versioned references to tokens, voice rules, claims, and owners. Mark dependent assets for review when a referenced rule changes.

Build a Python claim-proof ledger recording exact wording, scope, evidence, date, limitations, and review status. Use a fictional case or sources you can inspect.

## Use It

Ask Claude to propose a refresh and explain why it is needed. Apply one approved classroom change and inspect the impact on earlier work.

Ask Claude to challenge the scope of each purpose claim. Rewrite unsupported language or mark the claim unusable pending evidence. Treat legal and certification questions as unresolved unless appropriately reviewed.

## Ship It

Submit versioned brand guidelines, asset dependencies, change record, and updated specimen; claim-proof map, revised copy, checker, and limitations. Include the implementation, tests, inputs, actual outputs, source credits, and [submission records](../../templates/README.md). Post the final checked version to the designated GitHub location and upload the matching version to Canvas.

## Verify

Test a changed token affecting two assets, an unchanged asset, and an unknown version. A passing manifest does not prove the change is strategically wise.

Test evidence for a narrow product being used to support a whole-company claim. A nonempty source field is not proof. The exercise does not certify legal compliance or sustainability.

Document actual findings and limitations. Fictional cases, simulations, and proposed campaigns must remain labeled. No ad spend, public release, invented testimonial, or legal clearance is required or implied.

## Division of labor

**AI should** identify dependencies and draft consistent revisions; locate candidate support and propose qualified wording.

**Human should** decide whether to refresh or rebrand and approve the tradeoff; inspect the evidence, decide which claims are defensible, and own the commitments.
