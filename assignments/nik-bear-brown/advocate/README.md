# Advocate-role portfolio

## Executive summary

**What this is.** The working folder for my search for a Developer / Designer Advocate or technical-educator role I can hold while staying a professor — remote, part-time, consulting, or educational-material work at a design-tool or AI company. It holds the target list, my reasons for keeping each role, and (as they are made) the materials those postings ask for.

**Why read it.** If you are hiring for one of these roles: the target list tells you which posting brought me here and the reason under its card tells you why I think I fit and where I know I do not. If you are a student in INFO 7375: this is the same Part 1 you are doing, carried through to a real application — and proof that the professor's résumé produces gaps too.

**Where it stands (September 19, 2026).** Seven companies searched, six roles kept across four of them, one of which (Webflow's Senior Developer Educator) is US-remote. No part-time or consulting education role exists at any of the seven today. Materials are next.
